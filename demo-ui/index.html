Questions

Before you hand over the wheel

Can I watch the agent while it works? ⌄
Yes — the Agent Control Room shows a live browser window plus a side panel narrating every decision as it happens. Nothing runs invisibly.
What happens when LinkedIn asks me to log in? ⌄
The agent pauses and hands control to you directly inside the same window. Once you've logged in, it picks up exactly where it left off.
Can I stop it mid-run? ⌄
Always. The stop control is visible on every screen of the Control Room and takes effect immediately.
Will it apply to jobs I wouldn't want? ⌄
No — it only applies within the role, location, and salary preferences you set, and skips anything outside that range with a logged reason.
